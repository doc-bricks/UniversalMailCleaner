2. TARGET PERSONAS & VALUE PROPOSITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persona 1: Privacy-Conscious Professionals & GDPR Officers
  * Pain: Need inbox hygiene, data retention compliance, and newsletter purges without
    routing sensitive business or client emails through third-party cloud aggregators.
  * Solution: 100% Local-First Execution (INV-LOCAL-01) with encrypted OS Keyring isolation
    and zero telemetry or data harvesting.
- Persona 2: Storage-Constrained Gmail & IMAP Account Holders
  * Pain: Hitting storage limits on Gmail (15GB shared quota) or corporate IMAP accounts;
    faced with expensive monthly cloud storage upsells.
  * Solution: Tabular multi-criteria scanner for large emails and Drive files, freeing
    gigabytes locally with rule-based precision.
- Persona 3: Power Users & Digital Minimalists
  * Pain: Managing multiple email inboxes (GMX, Outlook, Gmail, Web.de) cluttered with
    thousands of obsolete marketing mails and notifications.
  * Solution: Multi-account switching, background scheduler maintenance, and safe trash mode
    with instant undo safety net.
- Persona 4: Solo Developers & System Administrators
  * Pain: Commercial mailbox cleanup tools are subscription SaaS traps that harvest email
    metadata or sell unsubscribe lead lists.
  * Solution: Free, open-source MIT Python desktop utility with transparent code, lazy dependency
    loading, and secrets-free configuration portability.
